How to read this figure. Each point represents the mean physicochemical value for one WHO/Pango variant within one phenotypic cluster; point size is proportional to the number of contributing sequences. Use the selector above to display points alone or points with boxplots. The boxplots summarize the unweighted distribution of variant-level means within each cluster (median, interquartile range, and Tukey whiskers) and do not represent the distribution of individual sequences. Hover over a point for the variant, cluster, mean value, and sequence count.
